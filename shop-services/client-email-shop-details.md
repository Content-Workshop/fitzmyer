**Subject:** A few details about your shop for the new Shop & Services page

Hi [Client name],

The new Shop & Services page is coming together. It's built around your repair shop, and the section that shows off the shop is the one that will sell it, so we'd like to get it right. Could you send us the following? Short answers are fine.

**1. Shop equipment**
What major equipment is in the shop? For example: lathes, milling or boring machines, balancing machine, welding, cranes or hoists, test stand.

**2. Lifting capacity**
What's the largest or heaviest pump you can handle in the shop (crane or hoist tonnage)?

**3. Testing**
Do you test pumps after repair, for example pressure or performance testing? If so, briefly how?

**4. Shop basics**
- Shop size (square footage), if you'd like to share it
- Typical turnaround time for a standard repair
- Do you also do repairs on site at the customer's facility, or only in the shop?

**5. Photos (4 to 6)**
Recent phone photos are fine. Landscape works best. Helpful shots:
- The full shop floor
- A pump being taken apart or repaired
- A technician at work
- A finished pump ready to go back to the customer

**6. Optional, if you know offhand**
- Do you offer a warranty on repairs? If so, what are the terms?
- Which manufacturers are you *factory-authorized* to repair or service?

Feel free to reply in bullets or call me if that's easier. Once we have these, we'll finish the page and send it over for review by [date].

Thanks,
[Your name]
Content Workshop

If you'd rather send something shorter, the four items that matter most are equipment, lifting capacity, testing and photos.
